## SLIDE 11: 5 FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES
**[Displaying: 5 Fundamental Principles]**

Here are the 5 rules for success:
1. **Grill Me**
2. **Ubiquitous Language**
3. **TDD**
4. **Deep Modules**
5. **Design vs Implementation**

---

## SLIDE 12: PRINCIPLE 1 - GRILL ME
**[Displaying: "Grill Me"]**

Don't just give orders. Say "Grill Me." Ask Claude to find the holes in your logic before you write any code. Reach a shared conceptual design first.

---

## SLIDE 13: PRINCIPLE 2 - UBIQUITOUS LANGUAGE
**[Displaying: Ubiquitous Language]**

Use a `vocabulary.md` file. Define every term. If you and Claude don't use the same words, the code will be a mess.

---

## SLIDE 14: PRINCIPLE 3 - TDD
**[Displaying: TDD with AI]**

Write the test first. The test IS the specification. Tell Claude: "Make this test pass." It’s the ultimate feedback loop.

---

## SLIDE 15: PRINCIPLE 4 - DEEP MODULES
**[Displaying: Deep Modules]**

Simple interfaces, complex implementations. Deep modules are easy to test and easy for AI to "understand" because their boundaries are clear.

---

## SLIDE 16: PRINCIPLE 5 - DESIGN VS IMPLEMENTATION
**[Displaying: Design vs Implementation]**

You are the Strategist (What & Why). AI is the Tactician (How). Never let the AI make architecture decisions. That is your job.
